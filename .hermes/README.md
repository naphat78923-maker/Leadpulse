- **Exposure** — `tracked` (`git ls-files`), `ignored` (`git check-ignore -v`, rule + line cited),

- **Safety** — `safe` / `PRIVATE` / `UNKNOWN`. `PRIVATE` = contains or may contain live
  customer/business data. Per the brief, contents of anything marked `PRIVATE` were **not read**
  and are **not reproduced** here; the label records exposure only.

**Screening method and its limit.** Safety was assessed by pattern-counting only (email-shaped
strings, company legal-suffix markers, Thai company-suffix markers, currency symbols) plus, where safe to read, the file's
own declaration. Counts were never printed with their matches. **Limit:** a bare brand name with no
legal suffix, no email and no currency marker would **not** be caught by this screen. That is why
files whose subject matter is live data but whose screen came back clean are marked `UNKNOWN`
rather than `safe`.
