# VG Saveur delivery policy (confirmed by Pat, 2026-09-11)

**Status:** confirmed business rule. No CRM schema field is added; per-account coverage is confirmed
case by case. De-identified and commit-ready.

## The rule

- **Dispatch** is from our own factory in Bangkok.
- **Outside the usual delivery area**, delivery uses a **third-party cold-chain service**.
- **Samples use the same arrangements** as regular delivery.
- **Destination coverage and delivery terms are confirmed per account.** Nationwide coverage must not be
  assumed, and no delivery promise may be made before that confirmation.
- **We do not currently export.** Overseas accounts stay on hold for this sales pass.

## What this changes

- Serviceability stops being "unresolved because no policy exists". A domestic Thai account is now
  *serviceable in principle*, with coverage and terms confirmed per account before any promise.
- Accounts outside Thailand are **out of scope for this pass**, not rejected on fit. The three
  cross-border pilot accounts are parked, not disqualified.
- Any draft message may propose a sample or an order, but must leave delivery terms to a per-account
  confirmation. No invented prices, sample terms, minimum orders, or delivery promises.

## What this does NOT establish

- It does not establish that any specific address is covered, or what the freight, minimum order, or
  lead time is. Those remain per-account questions.
- It does not clear suppression for anyone, and it authorises no outreach. Each account still needs its
  own approval before a message is sent.
